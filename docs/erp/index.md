# Bizmitra ERP

Bizmitra ERP runs the paperwork of a business: what you sell, what you buy, what you hold in stock, what the tax authority is owed, and who is allowed to touch any of it.

This section is the canonical answer for how it behaves. One page per question, written once, kept current.

::: info Being written
Most pages here are placeholders today. Section overviews are real; the guides under them are being written and published one at a time.

Need an answer before its page exists? [Contact Bizmitra](https://bizmitra.io/contact). Every question we get moves a page up the queue.
:::

## How this section is organised

**One page, one question.** A page answers a single question completely, and nothing else links to a second copy of that answer. Where a topic belongs to two areas — a credit note is both a sales document and a GST document — one page is canonical and the other section points at it.

**Task pages, not screen tours.** Pages are named after what you are trying to do, not after the menu you found it under. Menus move; the task does not.

**Nothing published half-written.** A page appears when it can answer the question in full. Until then it carries a placeholder rather than a partial answer that would send you the wrong way.
